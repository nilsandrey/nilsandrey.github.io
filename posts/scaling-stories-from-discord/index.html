Scaling Stories from Discord

18 Jun, 2023

The Engineering & developers at Discord moved from MongoDB to Cassandra once to process billions of messages, now they recently published about migrating from Cassandra to ScyllaDB to process trillions of messages.

"177 Cassandra nodes to 72 ScyllaDB nodes

Latency to fetch went down from 40–125ms to 15 ms

Message insert went from 5–70ms to consistent 5 ms."

I'm interested in checking all these related materials:

  • Blog post from the Discord team: How Discord Stores Trillions Of Messages (Online discussions)
  • An expected video from Hussein Nasser about it.
  • Benchmark (from ScyllaDB): ScyllaDB vs Apache Cassandra.
